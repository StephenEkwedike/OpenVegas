How purchasing works.

Only packs marked available can be purchased with your $V balance. Packs marked "Not yet for sale" cannot be purchased. Topping up does not unlock a preview.

For an owned, available pack, choose Owned, then Equip. Run openvegas emote sync in your terminal with the same account to restore your library.
