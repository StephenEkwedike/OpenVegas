Start with the house.

No opponent to find. Pick a game when you want a break.

  1. Run npx openvegas-ai in your shell and connect your account. Approve the host integration. Your host may also ask you to trust the OpenVegas tools.
  2. After setup, start a fresh claude or codex session in the configured terminal. No separate game window is needed.
  3. Enter the play command above, choose Quick Play (vs House), then a game. Enter at least 50 $V within your balance; submitting a table stake places the wager.
  4. Play the round. Wins and losses update your wallet. Quit or Esc returns to coding. A placed bet is not refunded; quitting an unfinished table hand forfeits it.

Sign in. Stay in the flow.

Your browser handles login and checkout. Your terminal keeps your place.

  • Choose Sign in/up in the play menu. Sign in in your browser, match the code, approve game access, then choose Continue in the terminal.
  • Choose Purchase $V to open your personal wallet. Review and complete checkout in your browser, then return and Continue to refresh your balance.
  • Signed out? Games stay locked. Opening login or checkout never places a bet or charges your card.
  • Agent wallets are separate. Owners manage eligible agent funding in the Agent workspace; availability is shown there.

Pick your game.

  • ♠Poker

    Inspect three shared cards, choose Continue or Fold, then reveal the last card and show hands. No extra bet for each reveal.

  • ♥Blackjack

    Hit for another card, or stand to finish your hand. Split matching ranks into separate hands (up to four), or double a two-card hand for one final card. Split and double each add a hand stake. Split aces get one card each. Dealer stands on 17; an original blackjack pays 3:2, split 21 pays even money.

  • ◉Roulette

    Choose red/black, odd/even, or a number from 0 to 36. Then choose Spin. The result appears after the wheel stops.

  • ♦Baccarat

    Choose player, banker, or tie. The cards decide the result.

  • 7Slots

    One stake, one spin. Check the result before playing again.

  • ♞Horse racing

    Set a budget, choose win/place/show, then pick a horse. Selecting its displayed price places the bet; entering the budget alone does not.

The menu shows what your backend supports. House games work now on compatible installations; public release availability depends on your installed version.
