What counts?

A friend must open your invitation before creating their account, then verify their email and complete a successful session that OpenVegas can verify on its backend. Existing accounts, self-referrals and duplicate verified email identities do not count.

Local activity callbacks and a browser's "completed" message are not proof. Native-only host sessions that OpenVegas cannot independently verify do not count. The server decides eligibility, trial expiry and ownership.

Only approved, available emotes on the promotion's eligible list can be chosen. Trials stop authorizing access when they expire; claims do not enable store sales or change your AI subscription.
