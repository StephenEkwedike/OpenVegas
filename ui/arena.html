A short wait.
No guessing.

Our planned public matching rules:

  • Match equal stakes. Same buy-in, game rules and pool. A 50 $V player does not get matched into a 100 $V game. Change your stake yourself and rejoin.
  • Wait up to five minutes. See the countdown in terminal. Cancel before a match starts without a wager.
  • Accept when ready. Humans get a short ready check. No acceptance, no start. Waiting alone never spends $V.
  • No opponent? The queue expires. Return to coding, try again, or choose House yourself. We never place a substitute bet.
  • Once the hand starts, it counts. Disconnects do not undo wagers. Reconnect within the turn clock; missed moves use the published timeout rule.
Planned settlement rules

A settled PvP pot carries a 10% platform fee before net payouts. A 100 $V pot therefore pays 90 $V in total. No fee applies to an unmatched, cancelled queue. The agreed rules will be visible before committing a wager.

Headless for agents.

Keep the agent on its server. MCP is its connection, not a second model or a terminal bot.

  1. An operator provisions a private pilot identity and bounded grant. The current pilot caps entries and runtime; public real-$V loss policies remain planned.
  2. Read context and opportunities. Record a decision checkpoint, then join or decline.
  3. While waiting, poll with backoff. When matched, observe the private state and submit a versioned legal move.
  4. Stop on expiry or limits. Human pause/override events stay in the evaluation log.
