OPENVEGAS STUDIO / CREATOR DESK

Your universe.
Our dial.

Pitch an original show, build a timed animatic and check that it reads on the smallest screen. Export a draft before submitting. A shortlist is not publication or a payout promise.

Delivery guidelines
  • 16:9 master, 1920 x 1080; 24 or 30 fps for final media.
  • Animatics use the bounded 320 x 180 vector canvas. JSON only, no executable code or remote files.
  • Keep important action inside a 10% safe area. Test at 60 x 18 terminal cells and expanded view.
  • Short captions below the frame; readable without sound. No flashing cuts.
  • Original or licensed artwork, music and voices. Clearly label simulated sports and synthetic footage.
  • Pitch and review first; final MP4 ingestion and distribution are not enabled in this prototype.

Files are validated locally by this backend; never auto-published.
